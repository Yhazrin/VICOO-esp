Part 2 — 5-Minute Demo Video Script
0:00–0:20 Opening
Welcome to the VICOO system demonstration.
This video shows how VICOO helps users discover sustainable fashion products, verify supply-chain information, and connect purchasing decisions with environmental impact.
The demonstration follows a typical journey, from product browsing to traceability exploration and platform features.⸻
0:20–0:45 Homepage
We start from the VICOO homepage.
The homepage introduces the platform’s visual identity and guides users toward sustainable fashion discovery.
Rather than presenting products as isolated items, VICOO encourages users to understand each product’s material journey and impact.
From here, users can enter the shopping experience and explore impact-related products.
⸻
0:45–1:20 Impact Shop
Next, we enter the Impact Shop.
Users can browse products connected with sustainability or charity narratives. Product cards display images, descriptions, prices, and impact-related labels.
This section supports the first step of the user journey: discovery.
Now we open one impact product and its detail page.⸻
1:20–2:00 Product Detail Page
On the product detail page, users can see product images, descriptions, sustainability information, and impact statements.
The system presents the product as part of a sustainability chain. Users can inspect material sources, logistics stages, certification records, and carbon-related data when available.
This helps transform sustainability claims into information users can compare and understand.⸻
2:00–3:00 Traceability Globe and Timeline
Now we move to the traceability section.
The Traceability Globe visualizes supply-chain stages as interactive geographic nodes. Each node represents stages such as material sourcing, manufacturing, quality inspection, or logistics.
When users select or scroll through a stage, related timeline information appears below. This interaction helps users understand the supply chain as a connected journey.
For each stage, the system can display location, partner information, carbon-related data, and supporting evidence.
This is one of VICOO’s core features: transforming structured supply-chain data into a clear visual explanation.⸻
3:00–3:30 AI Assistant
Next, we demonstrate the AI Assistant.
Users can ask product or sustainability questions in natural language, such as product materials, traceability information, or sustainability labels.
The assistant works as a support layer over structured product information.
This shows how generative AI can improve understanding within a structured system.⸻
3:30–3:55 Donation and Charity Interaction
VICOO also includes charity-oriented interaction.
Users can view donation-related content and understand how products or campaigns connect with social contribution.
The key idea is transparency between brand activity and social impact.⸻
3:55–4:20 System Reliability and Deployment
Behind the interface, VICOO uses a FastAPI backend, MySQL database, Redis, and Docker Compose deployment.
The backend provides APIs for products, traceability data, campaigns, donations, users, and related records.
The containerized deployment supports consistent operation across development, testing, and assessment environments.⸻
4:20–4:50 Closing
To conclude, VICOO demonstrates how a sustainable fashion platform can combine product browsing, supply-chain traceability, visual explanation, charity participation, and AI-assisted guidance.
Future extensions may include partner submission, payment integration, and carbon verification.
Thank you for watching our demonstration.

0:00–0:20 Opening（开场）
欢迎观看 VICOO 系统演示。
本视频将展示 VICOO 如何帮助用户发现可持续时尚产品、验证供应链信息，并将消费决策与环境影响联系起来。
整个演示将按照一个典型用户流程展开，从产品浏览到 traceability exploration 以及平台功能展示。
⸻
0:20–0:45 Homepage（首页）
首先我们进入 VICOO 首页。
首页展示了平台整体视觉风格，并引导用户进入可持续时尚探索体验。
相比将产品单纯作为商品展示，VICOO 更强调产品背后的材料来源与 impact。
用户可以从这里进入购物流程，并继续探索 impact-related products。
⸻
0:45–1:20 Impact Shop（Impact Shop）
接下来我们进入 Impact Shop。
用户可以浏览与 sustainability 或 charity narratives 相关的产品。产品卡片会展示图片、简介、价格以及 impact-related labels。
这一部分对应用户流程中的第一步：discovery。
现在我们打开一个 impact product 的详情页。
⸻
1:20–2:00 Product Detail Page（产品详情页）
在产品详情页中，用户可以查看产品图片、描述、sustainability information 以及 impact statements。
系统会将产品展示为完整 sustainability chain 的一部分。
用户可以查看材料来源、物流阶段、认证记录以及 carbon-related data。
这些信息帮助用户更直观地理解并比较 sustainability claims。
⸻
2:00–3:00 Traceability Globe and Timeline（追溯 Globe 与时间线）
现在我们进入 traceability section。
Traceability Globe 会将供应链阶段可视化为交互式地理节点。
每个节点代表一个阶段，例如 material sourcing、manufacturing、quality inspection 或 logistics。
当用户选择或滚动某个阶段时，下方会同步显示对应 timeline information。
系统还可以展示 location、partner information、carbon-related data 以及 supporting evidence。
这是 VICOO 的核心功能之一：将结构化供应链数据转化为清晰直观的可视化说明。
⸻
3:00–3:30 AI Assistant（AI 助手）
接下来我们展示 AI Assistant。
用户可以通过自然语言询问产品或 sustainability 相关问题，例如产品材料、traceability information 或 sustainability labels。
AI Assistant 作为结构化产品信息的辅助层运行。
这展示了 generative AI 如何在结构化系统中提升用户理解。
⸻
3:30–3:55 Donation and Charity Interaction（公益与捐赠互动）
VICOO 还包含 charity-oriented interaction。
用户可以查看 donation-related content，并了解产品或 campaign 如何与 social contribution 建立联系。
核心理念是增强 brand activity 与 social impact 之间的透明度。
⸻
3:55–4:20 System Reliability and Deployment（系统部署与稳定性）
在系统架构方面，VICOO 使用 FastAPI backend、MySQL database、Redis 以及 Docker Compose deployment。
后端为 products、traceability data、campaigns、donations、users 等内容提供 API 支持。
容器化部署能够保证系统在开发、测试以及 assessment 环境中的稳定运行。
⸻
4:20–4:50 Closing（结尾）
总体来说，VICOO 展示了一个可持续时尚平台如何结合产品浏览、供应链追溯、可视化解释、公益参与以及 AI-assisted guidance。
未来版本可能会加入 partner submission、payment integration 以及 carbon verification 等功能。
感谢观看我们的演示。
